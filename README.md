## 💡 Overview

Smallholder farmers often face challenges accessing timely financial relief, agricultural inputs, market pricing, and expert guidance. **Gebere-Redat** provides a streamlined interface connecting farmers with agricultural organizations, government programs, and relief initiatives to ensure transparent, efficient resource delivery.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 👨‍🌾 **Farmer Registration & Profiles:** Manage farmer records, farm dimensions, and specific crop/livestock needs.
- 📦 **Resource & Relief Distribution:** Track agricultural inputs (seeds, fertilizers, equipment) and relief funds transparently.
- 📈 **Market Price Insights:** Stay updated on current localized market prices for produce.
- 🤝 **Community & Advisory Support:** Connect farmers directly with agricultural extension workers and experts.
- 📱 **Mobile & Web Accessibility:** Designed to work efficiently across web and mobile platforms with localized language support.
